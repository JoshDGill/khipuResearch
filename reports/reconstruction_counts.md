# Reconstruction counts

Generated by `python -m steward.reconstruction_counts`. **Source: OKR v2.1.0 and KBCR v6 only.** KFG reconciliation is not yet included, so tiers that depend on KFG repairs (the KFG reports ~703 well-formed khipus) are expected to be lower here.

## Definitions and results

"Reconstructable" means a khipu whose cords and knots can be loaded as a structure (T3). Each later tier is a subset of the one it names. Targets come from AGENTS.md §10 and are compared, not copied.

| Tier | Measure | Computed | Brief target | Definition and exclusions |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| T1 | Known surviving khipus | n/a | ~1,650-1,721 | Not computable from open data. Thompson (2025) census; KBCR lists 1,204 distinct khipus or bundles named in literature. |
| T2 | With any public digital record | 701 | ~650-703 | KH numbers issued by OKR (issued only for khipus with public data), minus duplicate records. |
| T3 | Cord-level data in khipu.db, structurally loadable | 611 | ~640 (range 619-703) | In khipu.db, not a duplicate record, ≥1 cord and ≥1 primary_cord row. |

## Notes

- 83 KH numbers are issued but have no rows in khipu.db (mostly post-2017 spreadsheets, PDFs and images in the OKR archive). They count in T2 only.
- T5 is limited by attachment. Of the T4 khipus, ply is ≥90% known on 444, colour on 572, knot direction on 451, but attachment on only 163. Without the attachment requirement T5 would be 429.
- T6 counts a khipu with even one knot outside S/L/E. Structural problems alone (no cords or knots, no or duplicated primary cord rows, suspect splice) affect 35.
- T5 sensitivity: 63 khipus at a 100% threshold, 324 at 50%.
- KBCR v6: 1,204 distinct khipu codes in literature, 643 with KH numbers and 561 without. The brief cites ~504 literature-mentioned khipus absent from open databases.
- The duplicate record KH0483 (= KH0140) is excluded from every tier.
