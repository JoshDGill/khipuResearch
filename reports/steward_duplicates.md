- **shared_accession:** khipus catalogued under one accession number are separate objects (often tied together). They are not merged, but they share a split group.
- **content_match_candidate:** distinct khipus whose non-zero cord values overlap with multiset Jaccard ≥ 0.8 (only sequences with ≥ 8 non-zero values and ≥ 4 distinct values are compared). These are **not** merged. They may be matched accounts (as described for Puruchuco), so they need human review and must stay on the same side of any split.
